# Metric Definitions

## Governed Metrics

### Revenue
Monthly service value attributable to the reporting period, net of refunds

### Active Customers
Active paid subscription and at least 1 session in trailing 60 days

### Conversion Rate
Qualified leads tied to a closed-won customer divided by qualified leads created in cohort month

## Department-Specific Definitions

### Sales Department
- Revenue: Annual contract value for opportunities closed won in period
- Active Customers: Customer master status equals Active or Trial
- Conversion Rate: Closed-won opportunities divided by all closed opportunities in close month

### Finance Department
- Revenue: Invoice amount less refunds for invoices issued in period
- Active Customers: Distinct customers with a paid invoice in the trailing 90 days

### Product Department
- Revenue: Month-end MRR for non-churned subscriptions multiplied by 12
- Active Customers: At least 3 sessions in reporting month

### Marketing Department
- Conversion Rate: MQLs that created an opportunity divided by all MQLs created in cohort month

### Executive Department
- Conversion Rate: Leads tied to a won customer divided by all leads created in cohort month
